Privacy policy

Draft. It describes what the app actually does – read off the code rather than taken from a template. What it does not replace: a look from somebody who carries the liability. What is marked like this is still outstanding. The German Datenschutzerklärung is the authoritative version.

Controller

The controller within the meaning of the GDPR is the person named in the Impressum.

What this is about

Jagdaufseher manages a hunting district: its facilities, tasks, game reports and the members of that district. An account is needed for that, and data is stored – on the device and on a server.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is passed to third parties for advertising, and there is nothing to object to, because none of that happens.

The same goes for these pages under /jagdaufseher/: they load no fonts, scripts or tracking pixels from third parties, set no cookies and therefore need no consent. They are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., San Francisco, USA – a Microsoft company), which keeps the usual server logs, including the IP address. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (operation and security). For the rest of the site at regetskcob.de, its own privacy statement applies; the Impressum is there too.

What data, and why

Account. Email address, password and a display name. Without them there is no sign-in and no way to tell who entered what. The password is not stored in clear text.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).

District entries. Facilities with type, condition and coordinates; tasks with due date and assignee; game reports with species, sex, age class, weight, place and time; the district boundary. Every entry carries who made it.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Photos. Only those deliberately added to a facility or a report. They sit in non-public storage and are reachable only by members of the same district. The app does not read the photo library, only the single image that was picked.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Location. Only while the app is in use, and only if the permission was granted: to show your own position on the district map and to suggest the place of a report or a facility. No background location, no movement profile. A location is stored only when it belongs to an entry. The permission can be withdrawn at any time in the system settings; the map keeps working.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent).

Technically unavoidable. Reaching the server produces an IP address and a timestamp there, as any network request does.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (operation and security).

Who sees the data

The data of a district is seen by the members of that district, and by nobody else. That is not merely a promise: the server enforces it for every single row (row level security), and a request without a membership gets nothing back. A guest in a district may read and change nothing.

Processors

Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage) – server location [enter the region; for the EU pick a region in the EU]. A data processing agreement is in place with Supabase.

Bundesamt für Kartographie und Geodäsie (BKG) – supplies the map tiles (TopPlusOpen). Loading a tile transmits the IP address to the BKG, as calling up any web page does. Tiles already loaded stay on the device and are not requested again.

Beyond those, none. The app embeds no service it does not need in order to work.

On the device

The app is built to work without a network. That is why the device holds: the district entries last loaded, map tiles, photos already viewed and – for as long as there is no connection – entries not yet sent, together with their photos. All of it lives in the app's protected area and goes with the app when it is uninstalled.

Retention

District entries stay as long as the district exists – they are its history. Account data stays until the account is deleted.

Deleting the account

The account can be deleted in the app itself: on “My districts”, in the menu at the top right, entry Delete account. You are asked twice; the second time the word “DELETE” has to be typed out. What happens then:

  • The sign-in account is removed – email address, password and display name are gone afterwards.
  • The entries in the district stay, the name on them goes. Facilities, tasks, reports and photos are the history of the district, not of an account; where a person stood before, nobody stands afterwards.
  • A district is never left without an owner: if somebody is still there, it passes to the longest-standing member. If nobody is left, the district is deleted along with it, photos included.
  • On the device the local cache, the queue and the stored photos are cleared before the app signs out.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), plus the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. A message to regetskcob@icloud.com is enough.

Children

The app is aimed at adults holding a hunting licence, not at children.
