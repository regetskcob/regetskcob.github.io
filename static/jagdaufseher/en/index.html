    01 — In development · iPhone · iPad · Android · Browser

    The district at a glance, signal or not.

    Box blinds, bait sites and feeders stand out in the woods – and what is known about them lives on paper notes, in group chats and in three different heads. Jagdaufseher keeps it together: what is out there, what needs doing and what has been reported.

    Ask about a trialWhat the app does
    02 — Features

    Four areas and one map.

    What a district actually needs – and one map that shows it all together.

    District
    District and boundary. Draw the boundary on the map and the area follows from it. Put a facility outside it and the app asks before saving.
    Facilities
    Every kind its own mark. Box blind, ladder stand, bait site, feeder, water trough, salt lick – with location, photo and the date they were last checked.
    Condition
    Fits the type. Anything holding a supply is full or empty. Anything built is sound or needs repair. The app only asks what makes sense for that facility.
    Tasks
    To a facility and a person. Refill a bait site, tighten a ladder, check a stand before a driven hunt: tasks belong to a facility and to a person.
    Game
    Reports and harvest. Harvest, found dead and roadkill with place, photo and date. The harvest count follows the hunting year from 1 April.
    Shared
    Several in one district. Several members per district, each with a role – from owner to gamekeeper. Everyone sees the same state.
    03 — Offline

    Works without a signal.

    There is no network in the woods, and that is exactly where the app is needed. So Jagdaufseher is not merely readable offline – it is usable offline.

    Opening
    A district opens straight away with the last state it saw, without waiting for the server.
    Entering
    New reports, facilities, tasks and ticks are kept and sent later – photos included.
    Marked
    Anything still on its way is marked as such in the list. Nothing disappears quietly.
    In order
    Once there is a connection again, everything goes out in the right order.
    In a browser
    At a desk the same app runs in a browser. The offline machinery does not apply there – that is what the phone is for.
    04 — Map

    All on one map.

    Facilities and game reports sit where they were recorded, with the district boundary around them and the map tiles kept on the device for offline use.

    Location
    Take the current one while creating an entry, or place it on the map.
    Boundary
    Tap it corner by corner – the area in hectares comes with it.
    Photos
    Belong to the facility and the report, not to the camera roll.
    Tiles
    Official German topography from the BKG, kept for the district.
    05 — Status

    Where it stands.

    Jagdaufseher is a private project and is not yet on the App Store or Google Play. Interested in trying it in your own district?
