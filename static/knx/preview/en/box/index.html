    Go ahead and press it: the box records every telegram, yours too.

    01 — KNX box · Raspberry Pi

    The bus records itself while you are away.

    Preview The KNX box is a Raspberry Pi that writes down every telegram around the clock and opens its own Wi-Fi. You set it up with the app: today with the Linux app, soon by QR code with iPhone, iPad and Android. No Home Assistant and no internet.

    Get the imageHow to set it up
    02 — Checklist

    What you need.

    About half an hour at your desk to prepare it, no Linux knowledge needed.

    Raspberry Pi
    Pi 5 or Pi 4 with a matching power supply (Pi 5: 27 W USB-C), details under Hardware
    SD card
    microSD of 16 GB or more and a card reader for your computer
    Computer
    Windows, Mac or Linux with the free Raspberry Pi Imager
    Network cable
    From the Pi to the router or switch the KNX IP gateway is on
    Gateway
    One free tunnel slot; Home Assistant or ETS often take one already
    App
    Today the Linux app, soon also iPhone, iPad and Android
    Printer
    Optional, for the sheet with the two labels and access details
    03 — Setup

    Prepare it once, then just pair.

    Image and label are made once at your desk. On site it then takes a cable, power and the app.

    Download
    Get the image (button at the top, file knx-box-….img.xz). Don’t unpack it, the Imager reads it as it is.
    Write
    In Raspberry Pi Imager: choose the device (Pi 4 or Pi 5), under operating system at the very bottom “Use custom” and the downloaded file, then the SD card. Answer the question about customisation with “No”: the box sets up its name, Wi-Fi and passwords itself.
    First start
    SD card into the Pi, connect power; no network cable needed yet. After a few minutes the Wi-Fi “KNX-Box-…” appears. Wait one more minute, then unplug the power.
    Read the card
    SD card back into the computer, print knx-box-en.pdf (German: knx-box-de.pdf). The sheet has everything: two labels to cut out (1 for the app on top of the box, 2 with the Wi-Fi on the bottom), Wi-Fi and box password, the link for the Linux app and the next steps. Keep it safe, then put the card back into the Pi. Next to it are the QR codes as images (knx-box-app.png, knx-box-wlan.png), the link as knx-box-link.txt and the values in knx-box.txt.
    Connect
    Network cable to the router or switch at the gateway, then power. After about a minute the box is ready.
    Pair
    In the app, “Add KNX box” and scan the label. The app joins the box’s Wi-Fi, pairs, sets the clock and looks for the gateway. If there is exactly one, it is set right away and the box records. Pairing with the app is part of Pro.
    With Linux
    Get the Linux app, unpack it and run ./install.sh in the bundle folder: it installs KNX Monitor to the app menu, then the download can go. On the computer, switch to the Wi-Fi “KNX-Box-…” (Wi-Fi password from knx-box.txt). In the app, “Add KNX box” and paste the contents of knx-box-link.txt; the app does the rest as above.
    Without app
    It works without the app too: the second QR code (knx-box-wlan.png) joins the box’s Wi-Fi, then open knx-box.local in a browser with the box password.
    04 — Data

    What the app puts on the box for you.

    Everything the box needs to know comes from the app. There is nothing to copy.

    Gateway
    The box finds it on its cable; if there are several, you pick one in the app.
    ETS project
    Import it in the app and it goes onto the box too. Then the box knows names, datapoint types and the time master.
    Keyring
    For KNX Secure the same: the box records secured telegrams readably.
    History
    The app shows it straight from the box, day by day, with replay and export.
    Storage
    On a USB SSD named KNXBOXDATA, otherwise on the SD card. At five telegrams a second about 43 MB a day, raw.
    05 — Recording

    Starts by itself, never stops.

    As soon as a gateway is set, the box records. There is nothing to start and nothing to forget.

    Always on
    One tunnel stays open, every telegram goes into a daily file, even with no device connected.
    Clock
    Without internet: the box sets its clock from the iPhone or iPad that connects, or from the KNX time master on the line (DPT 19.001, 10.001 + 11.001).
    Until then
    The box already records and puts the time right afterwards. Or it waits, if you switch on “Record only once the time is right”.
    Power cut
    After the restart the box reconnects and carries on; at most the last seconds are lost.
    Retention
    Seven days by default, up to ten years.
    Pause
    Frees the tunnel, for example when ETS needs the gateway’s only slot.
    06 — Connection

    IP today, straight on the bus tomorrow.

    Today the box sits on a network cable to the KNX IP interface or IP router. In future it is meant to sit right on the line too: with a bus terminal, no IP gateway at all.

    IP (today)
    KNXnet/IP tunnelling or routing, KNX IP Secure included. The box takes one tunnel slot on the gateway.
    TP (planned)
    A bus terminal on the line and a TP-UART module over USB (such as busware TUL). No gateway needed, no tunnel slot taken.
    Bus monitor
    Over TP the box also sees what a tunnel never shows: acknowledgements (ACK, NAK, BUSY) and repeats.
    Address
    To listen, the box needs no individual address and no ETS programming.
    Status
    In progress, see the issue on GitHub.
    07 — Hardware

    What goes into the case.

    Computer
    Raspberry Pi 5 with 2 or 4 GB; a Pi 4 works too
    Clock
    Recommended: the RTC battery for the Pi 5 or a DS3231 module for the Pi 4. Then the time is right even before the first iPhone.
    Power
    27 W power supply (USB-C); weaker ones crash under load
    Storage
    USB SSD or NVMe, 128 GB last for years
    The rest
    A case with a fan, a network cable, the two labels from the sheet; in future a bus terminal with a TP-UART module
    08 — Status

    A preview, to be honest.

    The image is built automatically and runs in the test setup. Nobody has tried it on real hardware on a real site yet. Reports are very welcome.

    App
    The box works with the app: setup, project and history go through it, the browser is only a stopgap (see Setup). iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows arrive in the coming weeks; until then use the Linux app.
    Gateway
    A gateway with a fixed address straight on the box’s cable does not work yet; that needs the switch.
    Wi-Fi
    The app joins by itself on iPhone, iPad and Android 10 or later; on Linux, Mac and Windows you join once yourself.
    Alarms
    Without internet there are no notifications to your phone.
    Feedback
    Bugs and experiences are welcome as an issue on GitHub.
    09 — Questions

    Common questions about the box

    Do I need a PC?

    Once at your desk: to write the image, read the SD card and print the QR code. Until iPhone, iPad and Android arrive, you pair the box with the Linux app; after that a phone or tablet is enough on site.

    Do I need Home Assistant?

    No. The box runs the same software as the Home Assistant add-on, just as a device of its own. If you have Home Assistant, the add-on is the better choice.

    Does the box take up a tunnel on the gateway?

    Over IP yes, exactly one, around the clock. Every iPhone and iPad in the box’s Wi-Fi shares that tunnel. If ETS needs the only free slot, pause the box. With the bus terminal (planned) this goes away.

    Do devices in the box's Wi-Fi reach the customer's network?

    No. The firewall keeps the box’s Wi-Fi apart from the network on the cable. Only INTERNET_SHARING=yes in knx-box.txt lets devices in the Wi-Fi out.

    Can the customer reach the box over their network?

    No. Over the cable the box does not answer at all by default, only in its own Wi-Fi. For your own box on your own network, turn on “Access via cable” in the app under Box settings, or set LAN_ACCESS=yes in knx-box.txt before the first start.

    What if someone walks off with the box?

    Whoever has the label can pair. That is why the app suggests setting your own box password right after pairing; after that neither the label nor the SD card pairs new devices. Under Box settings you change it later and revoke devices you no longer need.

    May I record the bus in a lived-in house?

    A bus recording shows when someone is home. Agree on it with the residents and keep the retention as short as you can.
