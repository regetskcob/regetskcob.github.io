    KNX Monitor · Delete account

    Delete account

    KNX Monitor · Account

    The account is optional. Deleting it removes your email address, your account ID, your device list and your stored plan at Supabase; your purchases are unlinked from the account at RevenueCat. Data on your devices (project, recordings, settings) stays there.

    In the app: Plus and Pro → Account → Delete account. This takes effect immediately.

    Without the app: Send an email with the subject “Delete account” from the address you signed in with to the address in the legal notice. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm it.

    Running subscriptions are not ended by deleting the account. Cancel them first in the App Store, on Google Play or via the link in Paddle’s purchase confirmation.
