    05 — Features in detail

    Everything you need on the bus.

    Without expert mode the app stays lean: the values, the telegrams and a check of your installation.

    Overview
    Overview & favourites. Every group address as a card with its current value and a mini history, sorted by the rooms and functions of your project. Charts are saved per installation and get their own section on the overview.
    Telegrams
    Live telegrams. Every telegram with sender, destination, value and anomalies. Search in the title bar, one filter button with counts, read and write values. Writing is an intervention in the installation and is your responsibility.
    Objects
    Rooms & objects. Commands and feedbacks grouped automatically, actuators drawn as DIN-rail devices with channel LEDs.
    Health
    Health. Reads status addresses, reports devices that went silent and checks cycles, values, datapoint types and bus quality. With a PDF report.
    Recordings
    Recordings & history. Record with a name and note; a long press keeps the last 10 seconds. Before a replay, change values, remove telegrams, save as a copy; replay with a timeline per group address, 1–60×. With Plus, long-term history and statistics with an activity heatmap.
    Secure
    KNX Secure & routing. Tunnel over UDP or TCP, routing via multicast, KNX IP Secure and Data Secure with the keyring from ETS.
    Comments
    On telegrams, charts and recordings, with up to three screenshots
    Exports
    One page for everything: telegrams as CSV and simple PDF in every tier, full PDF and ETS file from Plus, reports and a ZIP with everything
    Bug report
    Report a problem: reproduce the fault, tap each time it happens, and the diagnostic package goes to your electrician; opened, it shows findings and hints. For support optionally without names
    Getting started
    Teach in without an ETS project: press a button, name it, done. Without expert mode the live list shows every telegram as a sentence; plus connection help, a status light with weekly report and “Learn KNX”
    Backup
    Everything in one file with its own password, in the apps from version 1.9.1
    Demo
    Demo house to try it without a KNX system
    Languages
    German and English
    06 — Expert mode

    One switch, and the whole workshop is there.

    For commissioning, troubleshooting and handover. Turn expert mode on on the connect screen or at the bottom of the menu; in the Home Assistant add-on it is on from the start. Without it nothing is lost, the tools just stay out of the way. Charts and alarm notifications are part of Plus, bus monitor, diagnostics, commissioning, topology and cyclic sending part of Pro; try them all in the demo house.

    Bus monitor
    Raw frames with ACK/NAK, repeats and bus load
    Checklist
    Commissioning: a checklist of all status addresses, answers are checked off, PDF report with a signature field
    Diagnostics
    Read status, find programming mode, check addresses, scan lines
    Topology
    Map and topology: who talks to whom, lines, couplers and coupler traffic, tunnels
    Compare
    Bus traffic against the ETS project: what is missing, what is unknown, what never sends; plus the object flags (T, R, C) even without bus traffic
    Charts
    Up to eight addresses, one axis per unit, saved per installation
    Trigger
    Trigger recording for sporadic faults: starts on a value, a fault, a repeat or an unanswered read, with pre-roll
    Send
    Like the ETS group monitor: delayed and cyclic, more datapoint types (text, time, date, colour …), set a datapoint type per address
    Replay
    Play a recording onto the bus: choose addresses, change values, shorten pauses, then confirm explicitly
    Hop count
    Its own column in the live list, a hint on unusual routing counters, installation location next to the sender
    Connection
    Port, tunnel address, connection options and keyring right on the connect screen
    Alarms
    Alarm notifications as soon as an alarm or fault object fires
    Acceptance
    Ring-through with sound acknowledgement and an acceptance report signed on the device
    Fault hunt
    Catch sporadic faults over days, with the KNX box
    Photo docs
    Photos of the distribution board and labels with QR codes, per installation

    10 — Privacy

    Your data stays yours.

    KNX Monitor works on your network and stores on your devices. We have hardened the systems considerably, and the control stays with you: you need no account to use it, and the app does not send your project, telegrams or keys to anyone on its own. In the store versions it asks RevenueCat for your purchase status, even if you have bought nothing; the account exists for purchases, not for your installation. Read the privacy policy

    Local
    Project, telegrams, recordings and keys are on your device, your box or your Home Assistant, not with us. Your ETS project password is not stored
    Tracking
    No analytics or advertising libraries, no tracking
    Encrypted
    From version 1.9.1 the apps encrypt their files with AES-256-GCM, with the key in the system keychain. Without a usable keychain (Linux without libsecret), and for settings such as the gateway, data stays unencrypted; the add-on and the box do not encrypt their files themselves
    App lock
    Optional, with Face ID, fingerprint, Windows Hello, device code or an app password plus recovery key as PDF. It helps if the device falls into the wrong hands, not against malware under your user account
    Backup
    In the apps on every plan: everything in one file with its own password, in a format that is openly described. Nobody can reset the password, not even us
    No internet
    The add-on and the box need no internet. Data only goes to destinations you set up yourself
    Account
    Optional. It knows your email address, your devices and your plan, never your installation. Two-factor sign-in if you want it; export and deletion in the app, a removed device loses its session on the server. The data is at Supabase in Frankfurt, the contract partner is in Singapore, subprocessors are also outside the EU
    Network
    The app talks to your gateway and your KNX box. Exceptions: purchases (App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat, with a random identifier or account ID), the optional account (Supabase) and the QR scanner on Android (technical data to Google ML Kit)

    12 — FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    More help under Support

    Can I try the app without a KNX system?

    Yes. “Try the demo without a KNX system” on the start screen opens a sample house with a project and live values. Your own project stays untouched.

    What does KNX Monitor cost?

    The app is free and stays useful for everyday use. Plus costs €24.99 a year or €79 once, Pro €149 a year or €14.99 a month, with a free trial. The Home Assistant add-on and the Linux version are free for good; on Windows you buy through your account. Try everything in the demo house first, Pro included. Cancel a subscription any time in your App Store or Google Play account, on Windows via the link in Paddle’s email.

    Which KNX interfaces are supported?

    Any KNXnet/IP interface or IP router, for example a Gira X1. Tunnelling works over UDP or TCP; with an IP router you can also use routing, which needs no tunnel (not yet on iPhone and iPad, where multicast needs approval from Apple). USB and serial interfaces are not supported.

    Why can't I connect although the interface is reachable?

    IP interfaces only offer a few tunnels, often four or fewer. If visualisation, ETS or other apps use all of them, the interface refuses. Free a tunnel, configure an additional one in ETS, or use routing.

    Is KNX Secure supported?

    Yes: KNX IP Secure for the tunnel and KNX Data Secure for encrypted group addresses. Import the keyring (.knxkeys) from ETS. The keys are kept in your device’s keychain. If something doesn’t work with your interface, please report it on GitHub.

    How do I back up my data, and who can get at it?

    From version 1.9.1 the app can save everything from this device into one file with its own password (at least 12 characters). It stays wherever you put it, and nobody can reset the password, not even us. Not included are the sign-in, the app lock and the history on a box. Details are in the privacy policy.

    Do I need an ETS project?

    No, the bus monitor works without one, and you can teach in your installation without ETS. With a project you get names, datapoint types, rooms and better checks. The password of a protected project is only used during import and is not stored.

    How do I get the desktop version?

    Linux does everything the iPad app does. The download is in the GitHub release. Windows follows in the coming weeks; on the Mac, KNX Monitor comes through the App Store.

    What do I need for the KNX box?

    A Raspberry Pi 4 or 5, ideally with a USB SSD and a real-time clock (Pi 5: battery, Pi 4: RTC module), an IP gateway or the kBerry plug-on module, plus the app: on iPhone, iPad and Android in the coming weeks, on Linux today. The box also sets its clock from the iPhone or the KNX time master. The ready-made image is in the GitHub releases, everything else on the KNX box page; it is a first preview.
