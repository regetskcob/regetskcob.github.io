    05 — Features in detail

    Everything you need on the bus.

    Without expert mode the app stays lean: the values, the telegrams and a check of your installation.

    Overview
    Overview & favourites. Every group address as a card with its current value and a mini history, sorted by the rooms and functions of your project. Charts are saved per installation and get their own section on the overview.
    Telegrams
    Live telegrams. Every telegram with sender, destination, value and anomalies. Search in the title bar, one filter button with counts, read and write values.
    Objects
    Rooms & objects. Commands and feedbacks grouped automatically, actuators drawn as DIN-rail devices with channel LEDs.
    Health
    Health. Reads status addresses, reports devices that went silent and checks cycles, values, datapoint types and bus quality. With a PDF report.
    Recordings
    Recordings & history. Record with a name and note; a long press keeps the last 10 seconds. Before a replay, change values, remove telegrams, save as a copy; replay with a timeline, 1–60×. With Plus, long-term history and statistics with an activity heatmap.
    Secure
    KNX Secure & routing. Tunnel over UDP or TCP, routing via multicast, KNX IP Secure and Data Secure with the keyring from ETS.
    Export
    Export as PDF, CSV and for ETS (Plus and up)
    Bug report
    Diagnostics bundle for bug reports, without names or values
    Demo
    Demo house to try it without a KNX system
    Languages
    German and English
    06 — Expert mode

    One switch, and the whole workshop is there.

    For commissioning, troubleshooting and handover. Turn expert mode on on the connect screen or at the bottom of the menu; in the Home Assistant add-on it is on from the start. Without it nothing is lost, the tools just stay out of the way. Charts and alarm notifications are part of Plus, bus monitor, diagnostics, commissioning, topology and cyclic sending part of Pro; try them all in the demo house.

    Bus monitor
    Raw frames with ACK/NAK, repeats and bus load
    Checklist
    Commissioning: a checklist of all status addresses, answers are checked off, PDF report with a signature field
    Diagnostics
    Read status, find programming mode, check addresses, scan lines
    Topology
    Map and topology: who talks to whom, lines, couplers and coupler traffic, tunnels
    Compare
    Bus traffic against the ETS project: what is missing, what is unknown, what never sends; plus the object flags (T, R, C) even without bus traffic
    Charts
    Up to eight addresses, one axis per unit, saved per installation
    Trigger
    Trigger recording for sporadic faults: starts on a value, a fault, a repeat or an unanswered read, with pre-roll
    Send
    Like the ETS group monitor: delayed and cyclic, more datapoint types (text, time, date, colour …), set a datapoint type per address
    Replay
    Play a recording onto the bus: choose addresses, change values, shorten pauses, then confirm explicitly
    Hop count
    Its own column in the live list, a hint on unusual routing counters, installation location next to the sender
    Connection
    Port, tunnel address, connection options and keyring right on the connect screen
    Alarms
    Alarm notifications as soon as an alarm or fault object fires

    10 — Privacy

    Your installation stays local.

    KNX Monitor talks to the KNX interface on your own network. No account needed to use it, no analytics. Your project, telegrams and Secure keys stay with you; your ETS project password is never stored. Only if you buy Plus or Pro is your purchase shared with the store and RevenueCat, so the app can recognise it. An account exists for purchases only: on Windows it is required to buy, elsewhere you only create one if a purchase should work on several devices. It knows your email address and your devices, never your installation. Read the privacy policy

    Cloud
    Your installation data never in the cloud; an account exists for purchases only
    Tracking
    No analytics, no tracking
    Network
    Network access only to your gateway and your KNX box; the exceptions are purchases (App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat, with an anonymous identifier), the optional account (Supabase in Frankfurt) and the QR scanner on Android (Google ML Kit)
    11 — FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    More help under Support

    Can I try the app without a KNX system?

    Yes. “Try the demo without a KNX system” on the start screen opens a sample house with a project and live values. Your own project stays untouched.

    What does KNX Monitor cost?

    The app is free and stays useful for everyday use. Plus costs €19.99 a year or €49 once, Pro €99 a year or €12.99 a month, with a free trial. The Home Assistant add-on and the Linux version are free for good; on Windows you buy through your account. Try everything in the demo house first, Pro included. Cancel a subscription any time in your App Store or Google Play account, on Windows via the link in Paddle’s email.

    Which KNX interfaces are supported?

    Any KNXnet/IP interface or IP router, for example a Gira X1. Tunnelling works over UDP or TCP; with an IP router you can also use routing, which needs no tunnel (not yet on iPhone and iPad, where multicast needs approval from Apple). USB and serial interfaces are not supported.

    Why can't I connect although the interface is reachable?

    IP interfaces only offer a few tunnels, often four or fewer. If visualisation, ETS or other apps use all of them, the interface refuses. Free a tunnel, configure an additional one in ETS, or use routing.

    Is KNX Secure supported?

    Yes: KNX IP Secure for the tunnel and KNX Data Secure for encrypted group addresses. Import the keyring (.knxkeys) from ETS. The keys are kept in your device’s keychain. If something doesn’t work with your interface, please report it on GitHub.

    Do I need an ETS project?

    No, the bus monitor works without one. With a project you get names, datapoint types, rooms and better checks. The password of a protected project is only used during import and is not stored.

    How do I get the desktop version?

    Linux does everything the iPad app does. The download is in the GitHub release. Windows follows in the coming weeks; on the Mac, KNX Monitor comes through the App Store.

    What do I need for the KNX box?

    A Raspberry Pi 4 or 5, ideally with a USB SSD and a real-time clock (Pi 5: battery, Pi 4: RTC module), plus the iPhone or iPad with the app, which arrives in the coming weeks. The box also sets its clock from the iPhone or the KNX time master. The ready-made image is in the GitHub releases, everything else on the KNX box page; it is a first preview.
