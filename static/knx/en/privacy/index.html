Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: KNX Monitor does not collect personal data. There are no servers, no account, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app processes stays on your device.

Controller

The controller within the meaning of the GDPR is the provider named in the legal notice.

The app (iPhone, iPad, Android, desktop)

No data collection by us

The app does not send any data to us or to third parties. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking libraries and needs no account. We do not run any servers for the app.

What is stored locally on your device

  • Connection settings of your KNX interface (IP address, port)
  • Data read from your ETS project or CSV export: group addresses, names, datapoint types, devices, rooms, links
  • Captured telegrams, recordings, long-term history, check results (health, commissioning) and your notes
  • App settings such as language, favourites and notification rules
  • With KNX Secure: the keys and tunnel passwords read from the keyring, so the app can reconnect and decrypt telegrams. They are kept in the system keychain (iOS/macOS Keychain, Android Keystore, Windows or Linux keyring), on this device only. A tunnel password entered by hand is not stored.

The password of a protected ETS project and the keyring password are only used to decrypt them during import and are not stored. You can delete all data in the app at any time or uninstall the app, which removes the data from the device.

Network access

The app only accesses your local network to communicate via KNXnet/IP with the KNX interface or IP router you choose and to discover interfaces on the network. iOS asks for the “Local Network” permission for this. There is no communication with the internet.

Notifications

Alarm notifications are created locally on the device if you allow them. No server push service is used.

Export and sharing

Reports (PDF), CSV and ETS exports are only created when you trigger them. Where they go is up to you, via your operating system’s share sheet.

App Store and Google Play

Downloading the app is subject to the privacy policies of Apple or Google. If you have agreed to it in your device settings, Apple or Google provide us with aggregated, non-personal statistics (e.g. install counts, crash reports). We have no influence on this and cannot identify individuals from it.

The Home Assistant add-on

The add-on runs on your own Home Assistant host. All data (project, Secure keys, telegrams, long-term history, recordings, settings) is kept in the add-on’s data folder on that host, is part of your Home Assistant backups (protect them with a backup encryption key) and is removed when you uninstall the add-on. The add-on records the bus continuously while it is running. Access goes through the Home Assistant interface (ingress) and is authenticated by Home Assistant. For notifications and for reading the KNX integration, the add-on uses the Home Assistant API on the same host. It does not send any data to us or to third parties.

Support via GitHub Issues

Support and feedback go through GitHub Issues. An issue is publicly visible and requires a GitHub account; processing there is governed by GitHub’s privacy statement. We use what you write in an issue only to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Please don’t post confidential data such as passwords or your ETS project there.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since the app itself does not send any data to us, we hold no data about your use of the app.
